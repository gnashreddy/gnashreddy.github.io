About Me.

I'm a Software Engineer with a Bachelor's in Computer Science and a Master's in Cybersecurity from NYIT Vancouver. I spent three years at Newgen Software Technologies in India — joining as an intern and growing into a Senior Software Engineer building production-grade banking workflows.

My role was officially technical, but I voluntarily took on techno-functional responsibilities because I wanted to understand the full picture — how requirements are written, how clients think, and how the two worlds connect. I became the bridge between business and engineering, and it made me a better developer.

I moved to Vancouver to build my career here long-term. I'm driven by ownership, calm under pressure, and genuinely excited by complex systems — whether that's a banking workflow that can't fail, or a retail inventory platform that needs to be accurate across thousands of stores.
